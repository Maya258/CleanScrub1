About Clean Scrub

Clean Scrub is a proudly local car wash in Gqeberha, dedicated to keeping your vehicle looking its absolute best. We believe a clean car is more than just appearance—it's about care, pride, and attention to detail.

Our trained team uses safe, high-quality products and proven techniques to protect your paintwork while delivering a spotless finish every time. From quick washes to full valet services, we treat every vehicle as if it were our own.

At Clean Scrub, we don’t just wash cars—we pamper them with love.
